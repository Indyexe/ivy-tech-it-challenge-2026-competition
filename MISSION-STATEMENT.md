# Responsible AI Guide Mission Statement

Our mission is to give Ivy Tech students one place to check whether their course allows a specific use of artificial intelligence and understand what to do next. AI policies can differ from course to course. Students who are new to AI need clear guidance before they begin an assignment or submit their work.

Through the Responsible AI Guide in our student hub, students can select a course, ask a question about an assignment, and get an answer based on the relevant syllabus policy. The guide points students to the policy and offers a practical tip, such as how to document AI use when disclosure is required. When the policy does not address their question, the guide helps them ask their instructor for clarification.

The guide supports Ivy Tech’s goal of consistent academic quality across learning modalities by making course expectations easier to understand in person, online, and in hybrid classes. We want students to recognize when AI use is permitted, follow any conditions, and seek help when they are unsure. With practice, students can approach new assignments with greater confidence and take responsibility for the work they submit.
